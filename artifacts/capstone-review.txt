INTRODUCTION



Barangay Health Centers play a vital role in providing primary healthcare services, particularly for mothers and infants. These services include prenatal and postnatal care, immunization programs, growth monitoring, and health education. However, many barangay health centers still rely on paper-based record-keeping systems for documenting maternal and infant health information. Manual records are vulnerable to loss, damage, and misplacement, making it difficult for healthcare personnel to efficiently track patient information, monitor vaccination schedules, and generate accurate reports. As a result, delays in service delivery and challenges in healthcare management may occur.
With the continuous advancement of information technology, digital systems have become essential tools in improving the efficiency and reliability of health information management. A Barangay Health Management System can centralize maternal and infant health records, allowing healthcare personnel to securely store, update, retrieve, and monitor health information. Through digitization, health records become more organized, accessible, and protected, reducing paperwork while improving the accuracy and timeliness of healthcare services.
This study focuses on the digitization of infant and maternal health records through a Web-Based Barangay Health Management System. The proposed system aims to support healthcare personnel in managing health records more efficiently while improving healthcare service delivery at the barangay level. The system will provide digital record management, immunization tracking, appointment scheduling, automated notifications and reminders, and report generation to support maternal and child healthcare monitoring.
In addition, the system will include health education resources and family planning information to promote awareness and assist mothers and caregivers in making informed decisions regarding maternal, infant, and family health. The proposed system will also incorporate a Healthcare Facility Locator that enables mothers and caregivers to locate nearby hospitals, birthing homes, pediatric clinics, and health centers. Furthermore, a Health Concern Reporting feature will allow mothers and caregivers to report post-vaccination side effects, maternal health concerns, and infant health issues directly to healthcare personnel. A MEDIMAMA Health Assistant Chatbot will also be integrated to provide information regarding maternal health, infant care, immunization schedules, family planning, and other health-related concerns.

Project Context
The Barangay Health Center in Barangay Bucandala II is a community healthcare facility that provides essential health services, particularly for mothers and infants. Despite serving a growing number of residents, the health center continues to rely on manual record-keeping practices. Maternal and infant health records, including prenatal checkups, postnatal assessments, immunization records, and growth monitoring information, are documented on paper and stored physically. This process makes record management time-consuming and increases the risk of record loss, damage, duplication, and retrieval difficulties.
The proposed system is intended for use by healthcare personnel and registered mothers or authorized caregivers. Healthcare personnel, including Barangay Health Workers (BHWs), midwives, and authorized health staff, will serve as the system administrators responsible for recording, updating, monitoring, and retrieving maternal and infant health information. Registered mothers and authorized caregivers will be able to access maternal and infant health information, monitor immunization schedules, receive reminders and notifications, report health concerns, locate nearby healthcare facilities, access educational health resources, and utilize the system

s support features.
Currently, the manual management of maternal and infant health records exposes important healthcare information to various risks, including physical deterioration of records, incomplete documentation, data inconsistencies, and delays in retrieving information when needed. These challenges may affect the quality and efficiency of healthcare services provided by the barangay health center.
This study focuses on the development and implementation of a Web-Based Barangay Health Management System for Barangay Bucandala II. The system is designed specifically for barangay-level healthcare operations and is not intended to replace hospital information systems or provide medical diagnosis. Its primary purpose is to improve health record management, support maternal and infant healthcare monitoring, facilitate communication between healthcare personnel and registered mothers or caregivers, and enhance the overall efficiency of healthcare services within the barangay.
Objective of the Study
The main goal of this study is to develop a Web-Based Barangay Health Management System for the digitization of infant and maternal health records in Barangay Bucandala II Health Center. Specifically, the study aims to:

1. Improve the management of maternal and infant health records by:
Allowing healthcare personnel to efficiently record, update, retrieve, and manage maternal and infant health information through a centralized digital system.
Providing organized and secure digital records that contain maternal health information, prenatal and postnatal records, immunization histories, and infant growth monitoring data.
Reducing paperwork and minimizing the risks of record loss, damage, duplication, and retrieval delays.
2. Enhance healthcare monitoring and communication by:
Allowing registered mothers and caregivers to securely access maternal and infant health records through the web-based platform.
Providing automated notifications and reminders for appointments, immunization schedules, and other important healthcare activities.
Enabling mothers and caregivers to report maternal and infant health concerns directly to healthcare personnel through the system.
3. Support healthcare service delivery and administrative functions by:
Allowing healthcare personnel to monitor patient records, appointments, immunization schedules, and healthcare activities through an integrated dashboard.
Generating accurate reports that support healthcare planning, monitoring, and decision-making within the barangay.
Maintaining organized digital archives to ensure secure, accurate, and easily retrievable maternal and infant health records.
4. Promote maternal and child healthcare awareness by:
Providing accessible health education resources related to maternal care, infant care, immunization, nutrition, and family planning.
Integrating a MEDIMAMA Health Assistant Chatbot that provides information regarding maternal health, infant care, immunization schedules, family planning, and other health-related concerns.
5. Improve access to healthcare services by:
Providing a Healthcare Facility Locator that enables mothers and caregivers to locate nearby hospitals, birthing homes, pediatric clinics, barangay health centers, and vaccination sites.
Assisting mothers and caregivers in identifying appropriate healthcare facilities for maternal and infant healthcare needs.

Overall, this study aims to develop a Web-Based Maternal and Infant Health Management System that will improve the efficiency of health record management and healthcare service delivery in Barangay Bucandala II Health Center. The proposed system seeks to provide a secure, organized, and accessible platform for managing maternal and infant health information while supporting healthcare personnel and registered mothers and caregivers through digital record management, appointment scheduling, immunization tracking, health concern reporting, healthcare facility location services, and health education resources. Through the implementation of the system, the researchers aim to contribute to the improvement of maternal and child healthcare services within the barangay.
Purpose and Description
The project aims to develop a Web-Based Maternal and Infant Health Management System for Barangay Bucandala II Health Center that is user-friendly, accessible, secure, and efficient in managing maternal and infant health information. The system will provide healthcare personnel with digital tools for recording, updating, monitoring, and retrieving health records, while registered mothers and caregivers will be able to access health information and receive important healthcare updates through a web-based platform.
Key features of the system include:
Digital forms for recording and managing maternal and infant health information, including prenatal and postnatal records, immunization histories, growth monitoring data, and vaccination schedules.
Organized dashboards and reports that present patient information, healthcare activities, and health statistics in a clear and understandable format.
Automated notifications and reminders for appointments, immunization schedules, prenatal checkups, and other important healthcare activities.
A health monitoring dashboard that enables healthcare personnel to track patient records, appointments, vaccination status, and healthcare services efficiently.
A search and record retrieval system that allows authorized users to quickly access maternal and infant health records.
A health education module that provides information regarding family planning, maternal health, infant care, nutrition, immunization, and responsible parenthood.
A Healthcare Facility Locator that enables mothers and caregivers to locate nearby hospitals, birthing homes, pediatric clinics, barangay health centers, and vaccination facilities.
A Health Concern Reporting feature that allows mothers and caregivers to submit reports regarding post-vaccination side effects, maternal health concerns, and infant health issues directly to healthcare personnel.
A MEDIMAMA Health Assistant Chatbot that provides information regarding maternal health, infant care, immunization schedules, family planning, and other health-related concerns.
The study proposes the development of a Web-Based Maternal and Infant Health Management System as a secure, organized, and accessible platform for managing healthcare information at the barangay level. Through digital record management, healthcare monitoring tools, automated notifications, and informational support services, the system seeks to improve the efficiency of healthcare operations and strengthen maternal and child healthcare delivery within the community.

Scope and Limitations of the Study 
This study focuses on the development and implementation of a Web-Based Maternal and Infant Health Management System for Barangay Bucandala II Health Center. The system is designed to support healthcare personnel, registered mothers, and authorized caregivers in managing and accessing maternal and infant health information. It covers maternal and infant health record management, including prenatal and postnatal records, birth information, growth monitoring, health assessments, immunization records, appointment scheduling, automated notifications and reminders, and report generation.
The system also includes a Healthcare Facility Locator that enables mothers and caregivers to locate nearby hospitals, birthing homes, pediatric clinics, barangay health centers, and vaccination sites. In addition, it provides a Health Concern Reporting feature that allows users to report post-vaccination side effects, maternal health concerns, and infant health issues directly to healthcare personnel. A MEDIMAMA Health Assistant Chatbot is also integrated to provide information regarding maternal health, infant care, immunization schedules, family planning, nutrition, and other health-related concerns.
The study will be conducted at Barangay Bucandala II Health Center from August 2026 to December 2026, covering the planning, development, testing, implementation, and evaluation phases of the proposed system.
The study is limited to the healthcare services and records managed by Barangay Bucandala II Health Center and does not include data from other barangays, municipalities, hospitals, or healthcare institutions. The system is intended primarily for maternal and infant healthcare management and is not designed to provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical services.
The effectiveness of the system depends on the availability of a stable internet connection. Poor connectivity may affect access to records, notifications, and other online services. Some users may also have limited digital literacy, which may affect system usage and data entry accuracy. Furthermore, the chatbot is intended solely for educational and informational purposes and shall not replace professional medical consultation, diagnosis, or treatment. Historical records that are incomplete, damaged, or unavailable may also affect the completeness and accuracy of the digital database.

Definition of terms
Barangay refers to the smallest local government unit in the Philippines where basic community services, including healthcare services, are provided.
Barangay Health Center refers to a local healthcare facility that provides primary healthcare services such as prenatal care, postnatal care, immunizations, growth monitoring, and maternal and child health services at the barangay level.
Maternal and Infant Health Management System (MEDIMAMA) refers to a web-based information system designed to digitize, manage, store, retrieve, and monitor maternal and infant health records in Barangay Bucandala II Health Center.
Digitization refers to the process of converting paper-based health records into digital format to improve accessibility, accuracy, security, and record management efficiency.
Infant Health Records refer to digital records containing an infant

s personal information, birth details, immunization history, growth monitoring data, and health assessments.
Maternal Health Records refer to digital records containing a mother's personal information, pregnancy history, prenatal and postnatal records, health assessments, and other related healthcare information.
Healthcare Personnel refer to authorized health workers, including Barangay Health Workers (BHWs), midwives, and health staff, who are responsible for managing, updating, monitoring, and maintaining maternal and infant health records within the system.
Caregiver refers to an authorized individual, such as a parent, guardian, grandparent, or relative, who is responsible for caring for an infant and may access the system on behalf of the child.
Barangay Health Worker (BHW) refers to a trained community health worker who assists in delivering healthcare services and recording maternal and infant health information at the barangay level.
Midwife refers to a healthcare professional who provides maternal healthcare services, including prenatal care, childbirth assistance, postnatal care, and maternal health monitoring.
Health Record Management refers to the process of recording, organizing, storing, updating, retrieving, and monitoring maternal and infant health information using the digital system.
Immunization Records refer to digital documentation of vaccines administered to mothers and infants, including vaccine details, dates of administration, and immunization schedules.
Prenatal Checkup refers to a healthcare examination conducted during pregnancy to monitor the health and development of both the mother and the unborn child.
Postnatal Checkup refers to a healthcare examination conducted after childbirth to monitor the recovery of the mother and the health condition of the infant.
Appointment Scheduling refers to the system feature that allows healthcare personnel to manage healthcare appointments and enables users to receive reminders regarding scheduled healthcare services.
Healthcare Facility Locator refers to a system feature that helps mothers and caregivers locate nearby hospitals, birthing homes, pediatric clinics, barangay health centers, and other healthcare facilities.
Health Concern Reporting refers to a system feature that enables mothers and caregivers to report post-vaccination side effects, maternal health concerns, or infant health issues directly to healthcare personnel.
MEDIMAMA Health Assistant Chatbot refers to an integrated chatbot feature that provides information regarding maternal health, infant care, immunization schedules, family planning, nutrition, and other health-related topics.
Notification and Reminder System refers to a feature that automatically sends alerts and reminders regarding appointments, immunization schedules, prenatal checkups, postnatal checkups, and other healthcare activities.

	Healthcare chatbots have become increasingly important in providing health information and patient support. A Framework for Developing a Maternal Health Informatics System for an Urban City in the Philippines

The system allowed healthcare workers to store patient information, identify possible health risks, generate reports, and receive reminders to support patient care. The system featured electronic patient records, risk assessment functions, automated health reports, and reminder tools to assist healthcare workers in making informed decisions and maintaining continuous care. Moreover, the study showed that using digitized maternal health records can enhance collaboration among healthcare providers, improve monitoring of high-risk pregnancies and support timely interventions. Patient-Centered Digital Health Records and Their Effects on Health Outcomes: Systematic Review

Brands ,M., et al. (2022) examine the effects of patient-centered digital health records on health outcomes across multiple studies. Additionally, high levels of patient satisfaction, feasibility, and acceptability were reported. The findings provided evidence for the adoption of digital record systems in diverse health contexts.

The system will serve as a digital platform where health workers can securely record and monitor essential health data of pregnant women, mothers, and infants. Through this system, manual paper-based recording will be replaced with a more organized and efficient digital process.
The design of the proposed system includes modules for patient registration, maternal record management, infant health monitoring, immunization tracking, prenatal and postnatal care recording, report generation, record updating, and a Health Education Module. s effectiveness, reliability, accuracy, and usability. Through careful planning and implementation, the proposed system aims to support barangay health workers in providing better and faster health services to mothers and infants.

The development of Digitization of Infant and Maternal Health Records through a Barangay Health Management System adopted the Agile model with iterative stages. This model was used because it allows the researchers to develop the system gradually by module, test each feature continuously, and make necessary improvements throughout the development process. Since the proposed system includes important functions such as patient registration, maternal health record management, infant health monitoring, immunization tracking, report generation, and record retrieval, the Agile model is appropriate for ensuring that each feature works properly and meets the needs of the intended users.
Figure 3 shows the graphical illustration of the Agile model.




Figure 3. Agile Methodology





Planning. Identify the system goals, target users, and key features of the system, including patient registration, heal

th record management, alert notifications, and essential system functions such as real-time data access and communication support for efficient healthcare services.
Requirements Analysis. Gather necessary information from health workers, mothers, and caregivers in Barangay Bucandala II to define the functional and non-functional requirements of the system, including health record management, immunization tracking, scheduling, and system usability.
Design. Prepare the graphical user interface, the database structure, use case diagrams and create flowcharts and the system architecture. The goal is to build a digital platform that is both user-friendly and well-organized. This platform will help health workers manage maternal and infant health records efficiently.
Development / Iteration. Develop the system in modules to ensure organized and efficient implementation. The development is divided into iterations:
First iteration: Register/Login
Second iteration: Maternal Record Management
Third iteration: Infant Health Record Management
Fourth iteration: Immunization Tracking and Scheduling
Fifth iteration: Report Generation and Record Retrieval
Testing. Test each module to ensure it functions properly, including registration, record management, data retrieval and over system performance, especially the emergency alert and location functions.
Deployment. Implement the system for actual use by health workers and authorized users.
Maintenance. Maintain the system by fixing issues, improve features, and update the system based on feedback to ensure continuous efficiency and reliability. 
System Testing
The system will be tested to determine whether Digitization of Infant and Maternal Health Records through a Barangay Health Management System functions according to its intended purpose. The testing process will be conducted to check the accuracy, reliability, and performance of the system

It also aims to identify errors, technical issues, and areas that require improvement before the final implementation of the system.The system will also be tested to ensure that educational content, including family planning information, is accessible, clear, and user-friendly for mothers.

The respondents will be asked to use the developed system and answer an evaluation questionnaire based on their actual experience with the system.
